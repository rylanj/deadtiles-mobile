02 / The body

What keeps you alive

Hunger

Eat and drink

You wake up short on both. If either bar hits zero, you start losing health.

Weather

Stay warm

Open air, rain, and wet clothes pull your temperature down. A fire and dry layers pull it back.

Infection

Watch the bite

Untreated infection climbs. A course of antibiotics is what turns it around.

Skills

Work is the teacher

Strength, endurance, dexterity, intelligence, and charisma. Scavenge, craft, cook, fight, and barter to raise them.

Hands

Make it useful

Lockpick a door, cook a meal, read a page, or put up a structure. The county pays people who can do the job.

Gear

Don't get attached

Not even the clothes on your body are safe from other survivors.

03 / Factions

Three factions in Ashen County

National Guard

National Guard posts

They still have posts across the country. Birchport is the hub for Ashen County: barracks, hospital, and street patrols, with a few smaller outposts out in the county. They keep the roads and the town. The traders handle the goods.

Red Order

Red Order cities

City crews in red armbands. They take blocks, clear the buildings, and put survivors to work holding them. The National Guard does not run checkpoints on their streets. They live off what they seize, including Ashen caravans.

Ashen Traders

Ashen Trader caravans

A separate outfit from the Guard. They scavenge and buy from survivors, then move ammo, medicine, and food through the county. When a caravan is not passing, they keep stalls at National Guard posts, including the market in Birchport.

06 / FAQ

Common questions

What is Deadtiles?

Deadtiles is a free multiplayer zombie survival game you play in the browser. You scavenge, trade, and choose a side in quarantined Ashen County, a shared world with other players.

How do you play Deadtiles?

Open Deadtiles in the browser and sign in with Phantom or Solflare. The Android app can also sign in with Seed Vault. Stay fed, watered, and warm while you loot buildings, trade, and pick a faction.

Is Deadtiles free to play?

Yes. Deadtiles is free to play in the browser. Enter Ashen County from this site without buying the game.

What are Deadmarks (DMRK)?

Banknotes spend in Ashen County. Ship them to the wallet you signed in with, and one note becomes one Deadmark (DMRK) on Solana. The day's shipments share one pool, and better standing with the Ashen Traders gets a larger share. 250 notes is the minimum to ship.
