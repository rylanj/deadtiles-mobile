# Crafting

A workbench handles cloth, wood, and guns. A furnace handles metal, and only while the fire is lit. You find both. You cannot build them.

A recipe takes about 10 seconds. Crafting skill can cut that to half. The materials come out of your pack.

## Guns

Read a blueprint and that gun's recipe appears on the workbench. Until you read it, the recipe is hidden. A finished gun still needs the matching ammunition.

Kevin sells the sheets. The pistol is Neutral standing. The revolver and the shotgun are Warm. The hunting rifle is Friendly. The AK and the M4 are Allied.

## Taking a gun apart

Breaking a gun down at a workbench takes about 15 seconds. Crafting skill shortens that too.

If you have read that gun's blueprint, you get every part back. If you have not, you get 3 parts at random and the rest are ruined.
