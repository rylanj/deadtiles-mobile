# FAQ

## What is Deadtiles?

A free multiplayer zombie survival game you play in the browser. You scavenge, trade, and choose a side in quarantined Ashen County, a shared world with other players.

## How do I play?

Open [play.deadtiles.com](https://play.deadtiles.com) and sign in with Phantom or Solflare. The Android app can also sign in with Seed Vault. The first session starts in Birchport Hospital. See [How to play](play.md).

## Is it free?

Yes. You do not buy the game to enter the county.

## How do I get my health back?

It does not come back on its own. Use rags, a bandage, or alcohol, take multivitamins, or stand beside a lit fireplace or furnace. Doctor Clark at Birchport Hospital fully heals you for 50 banknotes. See [Survival](survival.md).

## Where is it safe?

Birchport's streets, and the inside of faction buildings, do not allow drawn weapons. Outside town, other players can kill you and take what you are carrying. See [Birchport](birchport.md).

## What happens when I die?

Everything you carried stays on your corpse for one hour. Anyone can loot it. You come back with basic supplies. Skills and standing stay. See [Death](death.md).

## What are Deadmarks?

Banknotes spend in Ashen County. Ship them east and one note becomes one Deadmark (DMRK). The day's shipments share one pool, better Ashen standing gets a larger share, and 250 notes is the minimum. See [Deadmarks](deadmarks.md).

## Can I work for more than one faction?

Yes, for now. Standing is tracked separately. Later jobs will force a choice. Attacking or stealing drops you a full level with that faction, and Exiled does not recover. See [Factions](factions.md).

## Where do I read this in game?

Open the Journal and the Field Guide. It is the short version of this book.
