# Deadmarks

Banknotes spend in the county. Ship them to the wallet you signed in with, and one note becomes one Deadmark (DMRK) on Solana.

Kevin runs the wire from the Birchport market. The marks go to the wallet already on your ledger.

Contract: [DMRKspYf38RCq9M6RSHP2CRhXVAPEkPLN9E7MwTfa95h](https://solscan.io/token/DMRKspYf38RCq9M6RSHP2CRhXVAPEkPLN9E7MwTfa95h)

## Shipping east

* The day's shipments are one shared pool of 120,000 notes. It resets on the UTC day. First come, first served.
* Your Ashen standing sets how many of those notes you can put on the truck.
* 250 notes is the smallest wire. If less than that is all the book has left today, that remainder can still go.
* Hostile and Exiled standing ships nothing.

| Standing | Notes you can ship in one day |
| --- | --- |
| Wary | 1,000 |
| Neutral | 2,500 |
| Warm | 6,000 |
| Friendly | 12,000 |
| Allied | 25,000 |

If the coast is full, or your own cap is used up, the wire stays closed until the next day.

## Buying notes back

Kevin also sells notes for Deadmarks, out of paper the coast has already set aside. Most standings pay 2 Deadmarks for one note. Allied standing pays 1 for 1.

Deadmarks buy notes, rare blueprints, and property deeds. The notes themselves are what you spend on food, gear, and Kevin's counter.
