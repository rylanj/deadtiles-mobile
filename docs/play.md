# How to play

Open [play.deadtiles.com](https://play.deadtiles.com) and sign in with Phantom or Solflare. The Android app can also sign in with Seed Vault. The game is free.

You wake in Birchport Hospital, short on food and water. Doctor Clark patched you up. The first session walks you through the rest.

## First hour

1. Walk. On a computer, click the ground or use WASD. On a phone, drag the stick or tap the ground.
2. Talk to Doctor Clark and ask what happened. Accept the work he offers.
3. Eat the tuna and drink the water in your pack. If hunger or thirst hits zero, you start losing health.
4. Open the map. Birchport's hospital, barracks, and market are marked.
5. Leave the ward and report to Captain Davis in the barracks mess hall.
6. Read the next job before you take it. That is how Davis says you earn the National Guard's trust.

When the tutorial ends, you are on your own. Scavenge, stay fed and warm, follow the story Davis offers, and build somewhere safe. Davis and Clark post more work on their boards. The Field Guide in your Journal covers the same ground as this book.

## What carries over

Skills, faction standing, and the work you have already finished stay with you. Gear does not, if you die before you stash it. See [Death](death.md).
