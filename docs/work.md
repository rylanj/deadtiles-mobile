# Work

The opening jobs come from Doctor Clark and Captain Davis. Read a job before you accept it. The Journal tracks what you took and what you still owe.

After the tutorial, Davis and Clark post more work on their boards. The Ashen Traders and the Red Order post their own. Pay is in banknotes, and finished work raises your standing with the outfit that gave it.

Some jobs send you into a named building in Birchport. Others send you out on the road. The map marks the ones that have a place.

Story items stay in your pack until the person who asked for them takes them. They are not for sale.

You can work for the Ashen and the Red Order at the same time for now. Later jobs will make you choose.
