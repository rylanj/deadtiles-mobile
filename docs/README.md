# Deadtiles

Deadtiles is a free multiplayer zombie survival game you play in the browser. The world fell to the dead. A few places still hold out, and everyone else scavenges what is left.

You share Ashen County with other players. Stay fed, watered, and warm. The factions you meet remember how you treat them. When you die, everything you carried stays on your body.

[Enter Ashen County](https://play.deadtiles.com).

The same rules live in your Journal as the Field Guide. This book is the longer version, for when you are not in the county.

## What you do here

* Scavenge houses, shops, and roads for food, ammo, medicine, and gear.
* Take work from the National Guard, the Ashen Traders, and the Red Order.
* Trade with Kevin and with other survivors.
* Build somewhere safe to keep your loot.
* Ship banknotes east as Deadmarks (DMRK).

Settlements are generated. Learn a block, then walk until the streets stop matching. Cities carry far more of the dead than a rural road.
