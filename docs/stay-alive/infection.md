# Infection

A walker bite can start an infection. So can raw meat. Antibiotics already in your system keep a new infection from taking hold.

Infection climbs on its own. Each point lowers your maximum health, so a bad infection leaves you with very little to lose. Antibiotics stop the climb and bring it back down. One course lasts a while. Medicine skill can stretch that course.

Doctor Clark at Birchport Hospital can fully heal you and clear an infection for banknotes. Bandages restore some health when you are hurt and need to back out of a fight.

Cook meat on a lit fireplace, and take it off before it burns.
