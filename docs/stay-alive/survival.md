# Survival

Hunger, thirst, and temperature are what kill you when the dead do not.

## Hunger and thirst

Both bars drain the whole time you are in the county. Thirst drains about twice as fast as hunger. At zero, you lose health until you eat or drink.

You wake up short on both. Cook raw meat before you eat it. Eating it raw can infect you. Burnt meat barely feeds you and dries you out.

## Temperature

Your core follows the weather. Open air, rain, and wet clothes pull it down. A lit fireplace and dry layers pull it back.

Rain soaks your clothes, and wet clothes keep far less warmth. Dry off beside a fire or indoors before it gets cold.

Sprinting and heavy clothes build heat. Slow down, shed a layer, or get indoors. Both hypothermia and heatstroke damage you.

## Stamina

Sprinting drains stamina. When the bar is empty, it only starts coming back after a short rest. Do not get caught sprinting with walkers behind you.

## Night

Walkers move faster after dark, and you see less. Light a campfire or get indoors.
