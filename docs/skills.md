# Skills

Work is the teacher. Using a skill raises it: fighting, searching, cooking, crafting, trading, and the rest. The Skills tab in your Journal shows what each level does.

Each skill sits under a parent. A sub-skill cannot rise past its parent, so Strength limits Melee, and Dexterity limits Lockpicking.

Reading a skill magazine trains the skill on the cover.

## Strength

Melee, Harvesting, Carrying, Construction.

## Endurance

Fitness, Immunity, Recovery.

## Dexterity

Accuracy, Crafting, Scavenging, Stealth, Cooking, Lockpicking.

## Intelligence

Medicine, Reading.

## Charisma

Bartering, Persuasion.

Bartering improves the price Kevin gives you. Medicine stretches a course of antibiotics. The rest show up in the Journal as you use them.
