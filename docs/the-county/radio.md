# Radio

Other players can help you, trade with you, or kill you for your gear. Outside Birchport, nobody stops them.

Carry a radio if you want them to hear you past shouting distance.

| Command | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| `/freq 3-3000` | Tune a channel |
| `/radio message` | Talk on that channel |
| `/ping` | Share your location with everyone on it |

Anyone on the channel can hear you. Pick a channel with the people you trust, and assume strangers on an open one can hear a ping.
