# Birchport

Birchport is the National Guard hub in Ashen County. They hold the town as a safe zone for civilians: barracks, hospital, street patrols, and a market run by the Ashen Traders.

Captain Davis runs the Guard from the barracks. Doctor Clark works the hospital. Kevin keeps the market stall.

## Safe ground

Weapons cannot be drawn on Birchport's streets or inside faction buildings. Town is where you heal, trade, and read the boards.

That protection is for people who follow the rules. Fighting players or guards in Birchport turns the whole garrison hostile.

## Theft

The National Guard watches the hospital and the barracks, and their dead wherever they fell. Searching their furniture or one of their bodies counts as theft.

If a guard sees you finish the search, the whole Birchport garrison turns hostile. Stop searching and they stand down.

The Red Order uses the same rule inside their own walls, but only the squad in that building turns. See [Factions](factions.md).
